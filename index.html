ectolink

Which ectomycorrhizal fungi are likely on a plant's roots?

ectolink is paused while the research behind it goes through journal peer review. The site returns, in full, once that work is published.

ectolink.org is built on the research of María de la Paz Mañé Duarte, whose thesis at the University of British Columbia, A roadmap to predicting associations between ectomycorrhizal fungi and host plants, charts a path for predicting which ectomycorrhizal fungi partner with which host plants. This site exists to make that work easier to find, read, and build on.
